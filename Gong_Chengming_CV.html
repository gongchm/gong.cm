Gong Chengming

gongcm@teld.cngongchm@gmail.com

Nanjing DERI-TELD, Chairman

TELD New Energy, Chief Scientist

His work centers on power-system operation and control, microgrids, vehicle-grid interaction (V2G) and virtual power plants.
The grid EMS and the microgrid/virtual-power-plant EMS whose development he organized are widely used.
